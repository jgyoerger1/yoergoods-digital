The brief

One page that holds a career, two degrees and a PMP, the tools I have built for running a technology portfolio, and the woodworking I do under the Yoergoods name, without feeling like a resume glued to a photo album. It had to load instantly, read well on a phone, and give recruiters and collaborators a PDF resume in one click.

What got built

  • A written design system. Colors, type scale, spacing and component rules in one file, so every section agrees with every other section and future changes stay consistent.
  • Large, confident typography on a near-black canvas, with tracked monospace captions. Content does the work, not decoration.
  • Seven sections, one scroll: work, education, skills, products, Yoergoods, off the clock, contact. A sticky nav keeps each one a tap away.
  • A woodworking gallery of forty resized photos that loads lazily, so the page stays fast even with that much imagery.
  • Search-ready and shareable: a proper title and description, an Open Graph image for link previews, structured data, and a custom domain.

Why it's here

Because it shows the method, not just the result. Writing the system down first is the same instinct as writing the project plan down first: decide, then build, then the build goes quickly and the result holds together. It is how every Yoergoods Digital site starts.
