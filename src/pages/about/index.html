Who you're working with

Project manager by day, builder by nature.

By day I lead enterprise technology programs as a professional project manager: plans, budgets, risks, and a lot of people counting on dates being real. I've spent eight years leading programs. I'm a mechanical engineer by training, I hold an MBA and the PMP certification, and on nights and weekends I'm a fine woodworker under the Yoergoods name.

Yoergoods Digital is where those things meet: craftsmanship, engineering and project discipline, pointed at small business websites. I treat a contracted website with the same excellence I bring to a multi-million-dollar program. Same written plan. Same weekly status. Same refusal to let a surprise reach the invoice.

If you've avoided agencies because the process felt opaque and the price felt like a trap, this is the alternative: one person, a clear plan, a fixed number, and a site that works harder for your business.

How I work

Run like a real project, at a fixed price.

Your project is smaller than the programs I run at work, but the discipline is identical.

01

One point of contact

The person on your first call is the person who plans, designs, builds and launches your site. Replies come from me, not a sales team, within one business day.

02

Scope, price and dates in writing

A 30-minute call, then a one-page plan with the pages, features, fixed price and dates. You approve it before anything is built.

03

Weekly status, in plain English

A note every week with a live link, even when it's just "on track". Risks are raised early, never invoiced later.

04

The number we agree on is the number you pay

No hourly surprises and no "phase two" you didn't ask for. If you want something new mid-project, I quote it separately and you decide.
