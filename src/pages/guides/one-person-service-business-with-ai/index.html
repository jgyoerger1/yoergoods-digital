Start a One-Person Service Business with AI.

Every "make money with AI" video sells a shortcut. This guide is the long way that works: pick a service real people pay for, deliver it unusually well because AI does the heavy lifting, charge a fixed price, and build a small business around it. It's how Yoergoods Digital exists, and it includes the paperwork I use to run it.

Who it's for

People with a skill and a day job who want a second income that is real, not a lottery ticket: a side business delivering websites, bookkeeping cleanup, resumes and LinkedIn profiles, local search setup, content, or admin support. You'll need a few evenings a week, a willingness to talk to people, and comfort with the word "invoice".

What's inside

  1. The honest mathWhat AI changes (your output per hour) and what it doesn't (trust, selling, showing up). Why that still adds up to a business.
  2. Pick a service you can deliver in a weekendSix candidates compared on demand, price, AI leverage and repeat potential. How to choose yours in an hour.
  3. Price it fixedThe deposit-and-balance model, why hourly billing quietly kills solo operators, and how to set your first three prices.
  4. The offer page in a dayOne page that says who it's for, what you do, what it costs and how to start. Built with AI, published for free.
  5. The first three clientsThey're people you already know. The message to send, the conversation to have, and the price to charge them.
  6. The delivery systemIntake form, checklist, AI-assisted production, your review pass. Consistent quality you can hand to a future helper.
  7. Paperwork that makes it a businessQuote, agreement, statement of work, invoice, care plan. The plain-English templates included with this guide.
  8. The portfolio flywheelEvery job becomes a case study and the next job's proof.
  9. Alongside a day jobTime, energy, boundaries, and the employment-agreement check you must do before you start.
  10. What's hype, and the 90-day planWhy "passive income with AI" doesn't last, and the realistic month-one, month-three, month-six milestones instead.

You walk away with

  • The offer-page template and the AI brief that builds it
  • Intake and delivery checklists you can adapt to any service
  • Plain-English contract, SOW, care plan and invoice templates
  • A 90-day plan with realistic numbers
