Website cost in Ohio, 2026 What Northeast Ohio web designers publicly charge.

On 11 November 2026, 17 web design firms in Northeast Ohio published a one-time starting price for a small business website. Those prices ran from $249 to $5,000, and the median was $1,500. Monthly care plans started at $29 to $150 a month, with a median of $49. Yoergoods Digital, a web design studio in Stow, Ohio, compiled these figures from each firm's own pricing page.

Reading the starting prices

A starting price is not the same product from firm to firm.

What the entry price buys

Three pages at one firm, eight at another.

The lowest starting price, $249, is for a three-page site. Of the eleven firms that state a page count, eight cover five pages and one covers up to eight for $2,000. Some include copywriting, stock photos or a year of hosting. Others list the domain, hosting and third-party subscriptions as costs on top.

What follows the entry price

Three of the seven prices under $1,000 come with a monthly fee.

Those fees are $30, $30 and $49 a month, and the $49 one is required for every site that firm builds. Of the other four, one says $0 a month, one is silent on monthly fees, one makes hosting optional, and one moves to paid hosting after the first year.

What was on sale that day

Some of the prices were promotions.

One $3,500 package is listed against a regular price of $5,500, and one $450 build against a regular $500. Using the regular prices instead does not move the median: it stays at $1,500.

What firms say is typical

Ohio web firms' own cost guides give ranges from $1,500 to $12,000.

Six Ohio web firms publish an article or answer on what a small business website costs. These are their statements about the market, not their own package prices, and they are kept out of every figure above.

  • Columbus area: $1,500 to $4,500 for a small service site
  • Columbus: $1,500 to $5,000 for a simple five-to-eight page site
  • Cincinnati: from about $1,500 for one to five pages, up to $6,000 or more
  • Delaware, Ohio: $2,000 to $10,000 for a custom small business site
  • Stark County: $2,500 to $5,000 for most Ohio small businesses
  • Cleveland: $2,500 to $12,000 for most small business websites

Northeast Ohio firms also describe a "typical" price on their sales pages, and those statements differ widely: $500 to $1,200 on one Cleveland site, $2,000 to $7,000 on a Stow site, and $3,000 to $10,000 on an Akron site. The national ranges are on the pricing page, with their sources.

Method and limits

How this was checked, and what it cannot tell you.

  1. Who was includedWeb design firms that say they are based in Northeast Ohio and publish a price on their own website. 24 qualified: 21 publish their own prices and 3 give only a typical range.
  2. Where they areAkron 3, Stow 2, Copley 1, Macedonia 1, Kent 1, Rootstown 1, the Canton area 3, the Cleveland area 6, Wooster 1, the Youngstown area 3 and Lorain County 2.
  3. How each price was confirmedA copy of each pricing page was saved on 11 November 2026 and every figure was found in the saved copy. Prices seen only in search results were dropped.
  4. Who was left outNational companies with city pages, directories and firms with no published price, 41 in all, plus 8 whose prices could not be confirmed on the page.
  5. What it cannot tell youIt is not a census, starting prices are not like for like, locations are as each firm states them, and prices change. Ask for a written quote.
