All Paws Pet Care

Two sisters-in-law with a five-star pet sitting business in Hudson, Ohio, and nowhere to send people except a Rover profile buried in an app.

The brief

The business already worked. Repeat clients, a perfect rating, photo updates at every visit. What it lacked was a front door: somewhere to send a neighbor that explained the services in thirty seconds and made booking a single tap. It also had to be done fast and cost almost nothing to run.

What got built

  • One page, built for phones first. Services, who they are, reviews and FAQs in one scroll, because that is how a pet owner browses at 9pm.
  • Every button goes to Rover. No duplicate booking system to maintain. The site does the convincing and Rover handles calendars, payments and insurance.
  • Reviews as the sales pitch. Real quotes and stats pulled from their Rover profile, so the proof does the talking.
  • Photos from their own feeds. Twenty real dogs and cats from Facebook and Instagram, resized and captioned. No stock photos of golden retrievers that have never met them.
  • Zero hosting cost. A static page with a one-click publish script. Nothing to renew, nothing to update.

The project manager part

The scope was written down in one sentence before anything else: one page, all roads lead to Rover, live today. Everything that did not serve that sentence was left out, which is how the page went from idea to live in a single day without feeling thin.
