Case study

Yoerger Renovations

A remodeling and handyman business in Kent, Ohio, that needed to look as good online as its basements, decks and kitchens look in person.

The brief

Ryan does beautiful work and had the photos to prove it, sitting on a phone. Customers found him by word of mouth and had nothing to look at when they searched his name. He wanted a site that showed the work, explained each service plainly, and made it easy to ask for a quote, without a monthly bill from a website platform.

What got built

  • Six service pages for kitchens, bathrooms, basements, decks, living rooms and handyman work, each written around what local homeowners actually search for, with FAQs and a hero photo from a real job.
  • A project gallery generated from folders. Ryan drops phone photos into a folder, names them with a caption, and the build turns them into resized images, project pages, filters and alt text. Fifteen projects at launch.
  • A quote form with photo upload. Homeowners attach pictures of the room; the photos are shrunk in the browser and emailed to Ryan with the request. He replies from his inbox and the conversation is already started.
  • Free hosting on a custom domain. Static pages on GitHub Pages behind yoergerrenovations.com. No platform subscription, no plugins to update, nothing to break.
  • Search-ready from day one. Local business structured data, a sitemap, clean titles and descriptions on every page, and photos with real captions instead of IMG_4412.jpg.

The project manager part

One owner, a written list of pages and features before any design, and a build that Ryan could follow with a live link. The hand-off was a two-page README and a pair of double-click scripts: one rebuilds the site from his photo folders, the other publishes it. He can add a new project without calling me.

Details that matter

  • Every photo is resized twice (large and small) and camera orientation is fixed automatically, so a sideways phone photo never ships sideways.
  • The call-or-text button is always one tap away on a phone, which is where remodeling customers browse.
  • Spam protection on the form without a single CAPTCHA puzzle for real customers.
