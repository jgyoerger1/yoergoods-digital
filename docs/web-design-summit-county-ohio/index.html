One price list

Same price in every town.

There is no Akron rate and no Hudson rate. These are the published starting prices and fixed packages for every business, wherever it is.

  • One-Page Website: $500 and up, with a typical turnaround of one to two weeks.
  • Small Business Website: $1,800 and up for as many as six pages, usually three to five weeks.
  • Custom Business Website: $3,500 and up for a store, a member area or a custom tool.
  • Website Tune-Up: $250 to fix the site you have, on the platform it is on.
  • Refresh Essentials: $500 for a new design on existing content, up to 5 pages.
  • Refresh Plus: $850 for the same refresh on 6 to 12 pages.
  • Portfolio Website: $500 and up, one page for a professional or job seeker, in about a week.
  • Brand Essentials: $300 for a logo with an icon version, a color palette and a font pairing.
  • Brand Identity System: $1,750 for a complete logo suite, a brand guide, templates for social media and a print-ready business card.
  • Care plan, optional: $49 monthly or $490 yearly.

These are introductory prices for my first clients. Once a price is in your written quote, it is locked for that project. Hosting is free, so the domain renewal and the optional care plan are the only costs that recur.
