How it works

Short, organized, and you stay in the loop.

  1. DiscoveryA 30-minute call about your business, your customers and the brands you admire or can't stand.
  2. DirectionsTwo or three distinct logo directions, each shown on real things: a sign, a phone screen, a business card.
  3. RefineYou pick one, we refine it together. Two rounds of revisions are included.
  4. SystemColors, fonts and the supporting pieces your package includes, all built from the chosen logo.
  5. DeliveryA clean, organized folder of every file, named so you can find the right one in five years.
