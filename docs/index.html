How it works

Run like a real project. Because it is one.

By day I lead enterprise technology programs as a professional project manager. Your website gets the same discipline: one owner, a written plan, weekly updates, and no surprises on the invoice.

01 Week one

Discover

A 30-minute call. What you do, who you want to reach, and what you already have: a logo, photos, a domain, an old site. I listen more than I talk.

You get: a plain-English summary of what the site needs to do
02 Week one

Plan

A written scope, a fixed price and a timeline, in one page. You know exactly what you're getting and what it costs before anything starts.

You get: scope, price and dates in writing
03 Week two

Design

Homepage first. You react, we adjust, you approve. Nothing gets built on a guess, and you never have to imagine what "modern" means.

You get: a homepage you've signed off on
04 Weeks two to four

Build & review

A weekly update with a live link. Watch it come together, leave comments, and see the plan tracked against the dates we agreed.

You get: a status note every week, even when it's just "on track"
05 Launch day

Launch & handoff

Domain, hosting, analytics and Google Business set up and tested. A short training video so you can make small edits yourself, plus 30 days of included fixes.

You get: a live site, the keys to it, and someone to call
